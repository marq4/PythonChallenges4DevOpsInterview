Task:
* Given a list of products, either return the most popular one, or if there's a tie, one at random from the most popular products.

* Input:
  + N: number of products. 
  + List of products, one per line. 

* Example: 
8
greenShirt
bluePants
redShirt
blackShoes
redPants
redPants
whiteShirt
redShirt
